## Brief

Your client, a startup founder closing a seed round, has read an old blog post about "angel tax" and is worried the round will trigger a tax bill for the company. They've emailed you: *"Is this angel tax thing going to be a problem for us? Our investor is paying a premium above what our balance sheet says we're worth."*

## Instructions

Write the reply email. The founder isn't a tax specialist — they understand "valuation" and "tax bill" but nothing more technical. Reassure them accurately (angel tax was abolished for this kind of investment) without overstating the relief as covering something it doesn't (it isn't retroactive, and it doesn't mean valuation stops mattering).

Keep it to about 150-200 words.
