## Brief

Your client is acquiring TargetCo for a total consideration (including an earn-out) of **Rs 2,500 crore**. TargetCo is an early-stage digital platform: its reported assets and turnover are both modest, but it has a large and fast-growing user base concentrated in India. The client has asked: *"Do we actually need CCI approval for this? Our target's balance sheet is tiny."*

## Instructions

Write a short research memo (400-600 words) addressing:

1. The **question presented**.
2. Why the target's small asset/turnover figures **don't automatically mean no CCI approval is needed**.
3. The **Deal Value Threshold** test and whether this deal's facts appear to meet it.
4. What you'd still need to **confirm** before giving a final answer (don't guess at facts you don't have).
5. A clear **recommendation** on next steps.
