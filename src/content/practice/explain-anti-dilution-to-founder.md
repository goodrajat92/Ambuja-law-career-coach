## Brief

Your client, a first-time founder raising a seed round, has just read the anti-dilution clause in the term sheet their lead investor sent over. They've emailed you: *"What does this actually mean for me? Do I need to be worried about this?"*

## Instructions

Write the reply email. Assume the founder has no legal background and limited startup-finance experience — they understand "shares" and "valuation" in a general sense, but terms like "weighted average" or "down round" mean nothing to them yet.

Keep it to about 150-200 words. The goal is for the founder to actually understand what they're agreeing to — not to demonstrate how much you know.
